## KQ5. Whose loyalty account can UR be transferred to (primary cardholder, authorized user, household member), and how can two adults pool?

### Takeaway
Per Chase's Rewards Program Agreement as quoted by OMAAT, partner transfers go only "to participating frequent travel program accounts belonging to you or **one additional household member who is listed on your account as an authorized user**", and once you send to that AU they become the only AU eligible. Moving UR points **between two people's UR accounts** requires only that they be household members (no AU needed). No 2025–2026 change to these rules surfaced.

### Cited Findings
- Agreement language (personal cards): transfers allowed only "to participating frequent travel program accounts belonging to you or one additional household member who is listed on your account as an authorized user"; 1,000-point increments; "Once you transfer points to an authorized user, that person will be the only authorized user on your account eligible to receive point transfers"; points are non-refundable once transferred and become subject to the partner program's terms. — [OMAAT, "Ultimate Rewards Transfers Restricted To Authorized Users"](https://onemileatatime.com/chase-points-transfer-authorized-user/) **[older post; OMAAT says the restriction "isn't new"]**; [OMAAT guide, "How To Transfer, Combine, & Share Chase Points"](https://onemileatatime.com/guides/transfer-combine-share-chase-points/)
- Household member "needn't be a spouse or domestic partner"; "move your Chase Ultimate Rewards points to the airline or hotel loyalty program of one member of your household who is also an authorized user on one of your accounts". — [Million Mile Secrets, "New Rules for Transferring Chase Ultimate Rewards Points"](https://millionmilesecrets.com/?p=149684) **[pre-mid-2025, potentially outdated]**; [Thrifty Traveler](https://thriftytraveler.com/?p=64537)
- UR-to-UR combining: "When you transfer your Ultimate Rewards points to another person's Ultimate Rewards account, Chase only requires they are a household member"; business cards also allow transfers to another owner of the company. — [Thrifty Traveler, "How to combine Chase points with another cardmember"](https://thriftytraveler.com/guides/points/how-to-combine-chase-points-with-another-cardmember/); [Bankrate, pooling card rewards with family](https://www.bankrate.com/credit-cards/rewards/guide-pooling-card-rewards-with-family); [The Travel Sisters](https://thetravelsisters.com/can-you-transfer-chase-ultimate-rewards-points-to-another-person/)

### Inferences
- Two-adult pooling for ORD→ICN, in order of simplicity: (1) **Combine UR→UR** between spouses' own Chase accounts (household rule), then transfer from one account to **that holder's** airline account — avoids the AU designation entirely; (2) add the spouse as an **authorized user** and transfer directly to the spouse's airline account — remember this locks that AU in as the only eligible AU recipient; (3) book both seats from one person's airline account (most airline programs allow redeeming for anyone), which sidesteps Chase's rules altogether. Program-side family pooling (e.g., Aeroplan Family Sharing, BA Household Account) is general knowledge, not verified here.
- Caution: if one spouse holds Sapphire Reserve and the other Sapphire Preferred, combine toward the **Reserve** account before any Hyatt transfer (1:1 vs 4:3; KQ7). For airline partners the ratio is identical on both cards, so combine toward whichever account can transfer to the intended member's airline account.
