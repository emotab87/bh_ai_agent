## Q1. Asiana per-partner deadline table (10/31, 11/30, 12/16 groups): which direction does it apply to, and what do the "Part 2"/FAQ texts say? (Gap 1a)

### Takeaway
The per-partner dates are **Asiana Club members redeeming Asiana miles on partner-operated flights (and upgrades)** — not the rules for United/Aeroplan/KrisFlyer members flying Asiana. Evidence: Asiana's notice frames the dates as "service end dates that differ by *operating airline*"; Asiana's 2026-09-30 customer e-mail (reported 2026-10-01) describes them as "코파항공의 마일리지 항공권 이용 가능 기한" etc.; PointsYeah reads the revision as "Asiana Club members can now book and travel on Copa… through December 16"; and the Turkish/Aegean/Avianca/Croatia exception (ticket by 2026-12-16, fly until 2027-12-16) is only meaningful for Asiana-mile tickets. For the opposite direction (United/Aeroplan/KrisFlyer/LifeMiles members on Asiana), the governing texts are the Star Alliance statement (travel completed by 2026-12-16, subject to each program's rules) and Asiana's FAQ (tickets for travel on/after 12/17 void; ticket ≥3 days before first departure). No notice titled "Part 2 (안내 2)" surfaced; the FAQ item is "Q6 항공사별 발권 및 탑승 일자" inside Part 1.
