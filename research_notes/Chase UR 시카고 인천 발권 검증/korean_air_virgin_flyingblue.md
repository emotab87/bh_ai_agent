## KQ3. Virgin taxes/fees on KE awards, booking fee, change/cancel, online vs phone, holds, booking window

### Takeaway
Cancellation (and effectively changes, which are treated as cancel + rebook) costs $100 for non-UK-origin awards (£70 UK-origin), effective Oct 30, 2024; Gold members cancel free. Virgin says KE awards are booked direct with Virgin, and 2025–2026 community reports describe both website bookings (with a calendar view that works intermittently) and phone bookings when the site fails; no evidence of a points-hold facility was found, and the booking window is 331 days.
