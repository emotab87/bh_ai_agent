## KQ1. Virgin Atlantic Flying Club × ANA award chart (Oct 2026): zones, ORD–Japan prices, Japan–Korea prices — verify/refute "ORD–Japan J 60,000 / F 85,000 / Y 32,500; Japan–Korea Y 9,000 / J 22,500"

## KQ2. Virgin Atlantic × ANA booking mechanics: one award ORD–HND–GMP or two? phone vs online, holds while Chase points transfer, fees/surcharges, change/cancel fees, infant price, booking window, finding space (united.com / Aeroplan / seats.aero), 2025–2026 partnership changes

### Takeaway
ANA awards through Virgin are **phone-only** (not searchable on virginatlantic.com); the standard workflow is find space on united.com (or Aeroplan/seats.aero) → call Flying Club → transfer Chase points (instant) during the call. **Hold policy is inconsistent across sources** (2024 reports: no holds; some 2025–2026 guides: 24–48 h holds; a May 2025 forum post doubted it) — do not rely on a hold. Virgin **passes on carrier-imposed surcharges** on ANA awards (≈$450 all-in was estimated on a first-class ticket after the June 2025 surcharge increases); infant on lap ≈ 1,000 (Y) / 5,000 (J) / 7,000 (F) points one-way per one source. Whether ORD–HND–GMP can be ticketed as one Virgin award, the exact change/cancel fees, and the booking window were **not retrievable** in this session.

### Inferences
- Because Virgin lists "USA–Japan" and "Japan–South Korea" as separate zone pairs, a Seoul destination will most likely be priced as the sum of the two zone prices (e.g., business 60,000 + 17,500 = 77,500 one-way per adult) whether or not the agent can place both segments on one ticket. **Whether a single ticket/PNR is possible is UNVERIFIED** (see Gaps). If it must be two separate awards, the Japan–Korea leg becomes a separate ticket with the separate-ticket risks in KQ6.
- Given the hold uncertainty, the safe plan is: (1) find NH space for all three passengers on united.com/Aeroplan, (2) pre-position only after an agent confirms the exact flights/cabin/price on the call, (3) transfer Chase→Virgin while the agent waits (instant), (4) ticket in the same call.
- Infant cash cost: because Virgin passes through ANA's carrier-imposed surcharges, expect the infant ticket to carry a share of taxes/surcharges in addition to the 1,000–7,000 points. Amount UNVERIFIED.
