## KQ1: How does United price awards in 2026? Partner vs own-metal, Saver levels, 2025–2026 changes, and observed ORD–ICN price points

### Takeaway
United has had no published award chart since 2019; United/United Express flights price dynamically with capacity-controlled "Saver" levels while partner (Star Alliance) awards are priced from an unpublished, largely fixed regional table that United changes without notice. Since Sept 2025, business-class Saver access on United metal has been substantially restricted to Premier elites and United cardholders, and since 2026-04-02 cardholders get ≥10% (≥15% with status) off United-operated awards (not partner awards). The only recent Korea-specific data points found are ~88,000 miles one-way for Asiana business (Upgraded Points, mid-2026, date not confirmed) and 100,000 miles + $46.83 for ICN–SFO business seen by a cardholder (milemoa, June 2026); Japan partner business was reported at 100,000 (elite/cardholder) vs 110,000 (others) in late 2025. No ORD–ICN-specific 2026 screenshots were retrievable.

## KQ3: Fees and policies in 2026 (booking fees, change/cancel/redeposit, no-show, partner rules, Excursionist Perk, booking window, holds)

### Takeaway
United's own FAQ says miles are redeposited on cancellation ("a re-deposit fee may apply" per the fee page), a $125 redeposit fee applies to no-shows for all members, name changes are prohibited, and award tickets are valid one year from issue; 2026 secondary guides consistently report $0 change/cancel/redeposit fees for everyone except the $125 no-show case. The Excursionist Perk ended for new bookings/changes on 2025-08-21. Awards open ~337 days out (secondary sources); no source on award holds was found.

### Cited Findings
